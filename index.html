About

Hi, I'm Athan

I'm a game developer at MeepMeep Games, a small indie studio where I worked on Galactic Vault (a movement-shooter roguelike released on Steam in March 2026) and where I'm now building V.O.L.T. Survivors, a 1-4 player co-op sci-fi bullet heaven.

I studied Game Development at Howest DAE, focusing on gameplay and systems programming in C++ (Unreal) and C# (Unity). Before games I did web development (HTML, JS, PHP), and I still love solving the creative and technical puzzles that sit between code and player experience.

Working on commercial games taught me what I enjoy most: enemy AI. Designing how enemies move, react and pressure the player, and tuning them until a fight feels fair but intense, is the part of game development I keep coming back to.
